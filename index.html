Why we made SecondGuessIt

Our Mission

Help people understand what AI can do, where it can go wrong, and when its answers deserve a second look.

Problem

Many people use AI every day, but most people do not understand how AI fails. AI systems often sound confident even when they are wrong, biased, or unreliable.

Solution

SecondGuessIt helps people learn AI literacy through real-world AI incidents. Players read a short scenario and predict what actually went wrong. Every story is based on a documented AI failure.

Why it matters

AI is increasingly being used in education, healthcare, finance, law, and everyday decision-making. Understanding its limitations is becoming an important skill.
