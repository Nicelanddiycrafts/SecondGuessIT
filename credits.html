People and projects behind the cards

Credits

SecondGuessIt is an educational project about the strengths and limitations of artificial intelligence.

AI incident data

Many incidents featured in this project were adapted from Failed ML, created by Kenneth Leung. Failed ML collects documented examples of machine-learning and AI systems that experienced failures, bias, unexpected behavior, or deployment issues.

Credit: Kenneth Leung
Repository: github.com/kennethleungty/Failed-ML
License: MIT License

Sources

Each card is based on publicly documented reporting, research, or investigation. Links to original material are provided after cards when available.

Project purpose

This project is intended for education. Its goal is to help people understand how AI systems can fail and why critical thinking remains important when using them.
